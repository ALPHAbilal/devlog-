# Research & Planning Documentation

This directory contains all research, planning, and strategic documentation for the Devlog project.

## Directory Structure

### 📁 pricing/
Pricing strategy research and analysis:
- `PRICING_RESEARCH_PROMPT.md` - Pricing strategy research framework
- `PRICING_COMPARISON_ANALYSIS.md` - Competitive pricing analysis
- `PRICING_STRATEGY.md` - Final pricing strategy decisions
- `PRICING_RESEARCH_CLARIFICATIONS.md` - Research clarifications
- `PRICING_STRATEGY_CLARIFICATIONS.md` - Strategy clarifications
- `PRICING_COMMUNICATION_STRATEGY_PROMPT.md` - Pricing communication approach
- `PRICING_UPDATE_SUMMARY.md` - Pricing updates summary
- `PROFIT_CALCULATOR.md` - Revenue and profit calculations

### 📁 prompts/
AI research prompts and answers:
- Various `*_research_prompt.md` files - Research framework prompts
- Various `*_research_answers.md` files - Research results and findings
- Various `*_PROMPT.md` files - Specific feature research prompts
- `EXPERT_CLARIFICATION_ANSWERS.md` - Expert consultation results
- UI/UX research prompts for specific features
- Marketing and positioning research prompts

### 📁 features/
Feature research and improvement documentation:
- `AI_RESEARCH_PROMPT.md` - AI feature research
- `AI_inspector_answer.md` - AI inspection results
- `AI_reserch_answer.md` - AI research findings
- `BLOCKCONTROLS_EXPERT_RESEARCH_PROMPT.md` - Block controls research
- `share-ui-improvements.md` - Sharing UI improvements
- `VERSION_TRACK_OPTIMIZATION.md` - Version tracking optimization

## Usage

These documents are used for:
- Strategic planning and decision making
- Feature research and validation
- Competitive analysis
- Pricing and business model decisions
- AI-assisted research and development planning

## Note

Many of these documents contain prompts and responses from AI research sessions. They represent the thought process and research behind major product decisions.
