# Video Assets for How It Works Section

Place your video files here with the following names:

1. **capture-demo.mp4** - Demonstrates the instant capture feature
   - Recommended: Show pasting code, saving a solution
   - Duration: 10-15 seconds
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 or 1280x720
   - Format: MP4 (H.264) and WebM for browser compatibility

2. **connect-demo.mp4** - Shows the connection/linking feature
   - Recommended: Display @mentions, linking related solutions
   - Duration: 10-15 seconds
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 or 1280x720
   - Format: MP4 (H.264) and WebM

3. **search-demo.mp4** - Demonstrates search functionality
   - Recommended: Show quick search, finding old solutions
   - Duration: 10-15 seconds
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 or 1280x720
   - Format: MP4 (H.264) and WebM

## Video Guidelines
- Keep videos under 5MB for optimal loading
- Use smooth animations and transitions
- Ensure high contrast for visibility
- Add subtle motion to keep engagement
- Loop seamlessly for continuous playback
