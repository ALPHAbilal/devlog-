DevLog

Design Concept

This logo combines the playful, developer-friendly aesthetic of GitHub's Octocat with the letter "D" from DevLog. The tentacles represent the interconnected nature of documentation and development logs, while the gradient green colors convey growth and innovation.

Key Features:

  • GitHub-inspired octopus design with "D" letter head
  • Six flowing tentacles representing connectivity and flexibility
  • Gradient from #10b981 to #0a7d57 for depth and luxury
  • Scalable design that works from 16px favicons to large displays
  • Animated version with gently waving tentacles for loading states
