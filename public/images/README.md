# Poster Images for How It Works Videos

Place your video poster images here with the following names:

1. **capture-poster.jpg** - Poster for capture video
2. **connect-poster.jpg** - Poster for connection video
3. **search-poster.jpg** - Poster for search video

## Poster Guidelines
- Resolution: Match video resolution (1920x1080 or 1280x720)
- Format: JPEG for smaller file size
- Content: First frame or most representative frame of the video
- Optimize: Keep under 200KB per image
- Style: Should give preview of what the video will show
